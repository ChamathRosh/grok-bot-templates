# Grok Bot templates for small businesses

**Grok Bot templates** are ready-made instructions you paste into a new, empty Grok Bot so it starts working on a specific small-business task right away. This repo holds 6 free, MIT-licensed templates from [WhatShouldMyBotDo](https://whatshouldmybotdo.com/?utm_source=github&utm_campaign=chanlab&utm_content=c98): a planner that picks your first AI agent, plus templates for chasing quotes, replying to missed calls, drafting review replies, nudging unpaid invoices and writing a morning brief. Every template **only drafts**: it never sends, posts, pays or contacts anyone without your yes.

## Which template should I use first?

If you're not sure, start with the task that costs you the most time each week and that is safe to draft:

- Quotes go unanswered: **Quote Chaser**.
- You miss calls while on a job: **Missed-Call Follow-up**.
- Reviews sit without a reply: **Review Reply Drafter**.
- Invoices are paid late: **Invoice Nudger**.
- Your inbox and calendar feel like a mess every morning: **Morning Brief**. It is read-only, so it's the lowest-risk start.

Still unsure? Take the free quiz at [whatshouldmybotdo.com/quiz](https://whatshouldmybotdo.com/quiz) (no signup), or install **What Should My Bot Do?** and let it ask you. The guide [What should my AI agent do first?](https://whatshouldmybotdo.com/guides/what-should-my-ai-agent-do-first) explains the reasoning.

## How to use one

1. Open the template's install link and choose **Add to Grok Bot**. Or, while a link is still "coming soon", create a new, empty Bot and paste the template's instructions as its first message.
2. Answer its first question or paste what it asks for.
3. Check every draft before you send it yourself.

## Rules every template follows

- Drafts only. Nothing is sent, posted, booked or paid for you.
- Nothing is connected without your yes, and never banking or payment accounts.
- No invented numbers, prices or promises. Missing details are left as `[FILL IN: ...]`.
- Safety, legal and money decisions stay with you.

## FAQ

**What are Grok Bot templates?** Saved instructions that set up a Grok Bot for a specific task. You install one from an x.ai link, or paste its instructions into a new, empty Bot.

**What can Grok Bot do for a small business?** Common use cases are follow-ups on quotes and leads, replies to missed calls and reviews, invoice reminders and daily briefs.
