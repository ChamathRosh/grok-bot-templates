# Small-business Grok Bot templates

Free, one-job Grok Bot templates for small-business owners. Each template does one job, and **only drafts**: it never sends, posts, pays or contacts anyone without your yes.

| Template | What it does | Install |
|---|---|---|
| [What Should My Bot Do?](templates/what-should-my-bot-do.md) | Free Grok Bot template: 5 questions about your business, then your first AI agent, 3 next jobs and a free guide for each. Drafts only. | [x.ai install](https://x.ai/bot/KOJbYHQwZmEacXcdpkdUZ) |
| [Quote Chaser](templates/quote-chaser.md) | Drafts polite follow-ups for quotes and estimates that haven't had a reply, for you to check and send. Drafts only: never sends, discounts or contacts anyone. | link coming soon |
| [Missed-Call Follow-up](templates/missed-call-follow-up.md) | Turns your missed calls and voicemail notes into short reply drafts for each caller and flags urgent ones first. Drafts only: never calls, texts or emails anyone. | link coming soon |
| [Review Reply Drafter](templates/review-reply-drafter.md) | Drafts short, honest replies to your customer reviews in your voice: thanks for good ones, a calm fix-it offer for bad ones. Drafts only: you post them yourself. | link coming soon |
| [Invoice Nudger](templates/invoice-nudger.md) | Drafts polite, firm reminders for invoices that are past due, from a list you give it, for you to send. Drafts only: never sends, charges or adds fees. | link coming soon |
| [Morning Brief](templates/morning-brief.md) | A read-only morning brief for owners: today's calendar, emails that need you, and what can wait, in one short message. Never replies, sends or changes anything. | link coming soon |

## How to use one
1. Open the template's install link and choose **Add to Grok Bot**. Or, while a link is still "coming soon", create a new, empty Bot and paste the template's instructions as its first message.
2. Answer its first question or paste what it asks for.
3. Check every draft before you send it yourself.

## Rules every template follows
- Drafts only. Nothing is sent, posted, booked or paid for you.
- Nothing is connected without your yes, and never banking or payment accounts.
- No invented numbers, prices or promises. Missing details are left as `[FILL IN: ...]`.
- Safety, legal and money decisions stay with you.
