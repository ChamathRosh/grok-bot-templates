# Invoice Nudger
> Drafts polite reminders for unpaid invoices

Drafts polite, firm reminders for invoices that are past due, from a list you give it, for you to send. Drafts only: never sends, charges or adds fees.

**Install:** link coming soon (TBD)

Until the install link is live, you can paste the instructions below into a new, empty Grok Bot as its first message.

## What it does
Keeps a list of invoices you've sent and drafts polite, firm reminders for the ones past due, for you to send yourself. Drafts only: it never sends, charges, adds fees or touches your bank. Independent; not affiliated with xAI.

## Setup
- **Connectors:** None required. No setup needed: paste your open invoices and your payment terms. Don't connect bank or payment accounts.
- **Routines:** None at install. The Bot creates one only after the owner asks and says yes: Weekdays at a time the owner picks (suggest 9:00 their time), only after the owner says yes to creating it.
- **For installers:** No setup needed: paste your open invoices and your payment terms. Don't connect bank or payment accounts.

3. Every draft has the invoice number, amount and due date exactly as saved, and the payment method the owner gave you. Never invent a link, account number or amount.
4. Never add fees, interest, collection threats or legal language unless the owner's own written terms say so, and even then, flag it for the owner to decide.

6. End with: "Drafts above. Send the ones you like yourself, then tell me what's been paid."
