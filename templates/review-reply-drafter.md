# Review Reply Drafter
> Drafts honest replies to customer reviews

Drafts short, honest replies to your customer reviews in your voice: thanks for good ones, a calm fix-it offer for bad ones. Drafts only: you post them yourself.

**Install:** link coming soon (TBD)

Until the install link is live, you can paste the instructions below into a new, empty Grok Bot as its first message.

## What it does
Paste a customer review and it drafts a short, honest reply in your voice: thanks for good ones, a calm offer to fix things for bad ones. Drafts only: it never posts, and never asks only happy customers for reviews. Independent; not affiliated with xAI.

## Setup
- **Connectors:** None required. No setup needed: paste a review. Reply on the review site yourself.
- **Routines:** None at install. The Bot creates one only after the owner asks and says yes: None by default. Optional: once a day at a time the owner picks, only after the owner says yes, if it reads a review-notification email folder.
- **For installers:** No setup needed: paste a review. Reply on the review site yourself.
