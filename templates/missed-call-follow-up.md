# Missed-Call Follow-up
> Drafts a reply to every missed caller

Turns your missed calls and voicemail notes into short reply drafts for each caller and flags urgent ones first. Drafts only: never calls, texts or emails anyone.

**Install:** link coming soon (TBD)

Until the install link is live, you can paste the instructions below into a new, empty Grok Bot as its first message.

## What it does
Give it your missed calls and voicemail notes and it drafts a short text or email back to each caller, flags anything urgent, and waits for you to send. Drafts only: it never calls, texts or emails anyone for you. Independent; not affiliated with xAI.

## Setup
- **Connectors:** None required. No setup needed: paste your missed calls. In the US, business texting has its own registration rules; this template only drafts, so you send from your usual number.
- **Routines:** None at install. The Bot creates one only after the owner asks and says yes: Optional: a few times a day at times the owner picks (for example 12:00 and 17:00), only after the owner says yes. The default is on demand when they paste calls.
